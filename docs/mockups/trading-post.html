    Before the first battle?

    Until the warband's first battle every warrior buys from his own list, rare items of the list included, at the list price — and founding prices apply (a Nightmare 30 gc instead of 95). From battle 1 on: common items here, rare items only when a Hero finds them (rulebook p. 46 and 104; Rob, 29.09.2026).

    Each Hero may look for one rare item: 2D6 equal to or above its rarity. Heroes taken out of action in the last battle stay home.

    What can be found

      Items sell for half the price that applies now (house rules included), rounded down but at least 1 gc; variable prices count only their fixed part. A group sells all its pieces of an item together. Wyrdstone sells by the table for the warband's size.

        Move an item between warriors and the stash. No gold changes hands; the price paid moves with it. Only those who may use the item can take it.
