Screens to try on the phone

The screens that are drawn before they are built (docs/ui.md §3). They are clickable, but nothing is stored or sent. Open them on your phone, switch between both themes at the top, and note what feels wrong in the hand: sizes, order, words, what is missing.

The campaign is invented: Rob leads and plays The Silver Caravan, Anna the Grey Penitents, Ben Clan Skrittle. Battle 6 at the Hel Fenn ferry is being played.

Start like the app: HomeThe desktop

From Home every place is reached as in the app: the bar at the bottom (Home, Warbands, Campaign, Notes, More) and the tabs of a warband and of the campaign. The screens below open directly.

  • Game night

    One hand, under time pressure, offline.
    • Are the four big buttons in reach of the thumb?
    • Is a casualty entered in three taps?
    • Is it clear what is still waiting on the phone?

  • Timeline

    Ordering the story after the battle.
    • Long-press a block and drag it; or use ↑ ↓ and “Move to…”.
    • Which blocks may move, which are anchors?
    • Are leader-only blocks unmistakable?

  • What changed

    Before and after, per warrior, with the reason found for each change.
    • Does a change without a cause stand out without shouting?
    • Is writing the explanations inviting?

  • Who can see this

    Public, sealed until the battle ends, leader only.
    • Can anything be revealed by accident?
    • Do the three states differ by more than colour?
    • Try “View as player”.

  • Background

    The leader’s hidden threads and how much may be hinted.
    • Is the reveal level readable at a glance?
    • Is revealing deliberate enough?

  • Roster

    The most used screen, rebuilt for the phone.
    • Is everything for one warrior on one card?
    • Are the common actions one tap away?
    • Henchmen: + Man, names of the men, the lad's got talent.
    • Advance with a new skill: every skill of his lists, with what it does.

  • Home

    What is open for you, your warbands, your campaigns.
    • Is the first thing you see the thing you would do next?
    • The city at night: pixel art, still if the phone asks for less motion. On one visit in three the twin-tailed comet comes down (home.html?impact=1 shows it every time).
    • The warbands' emblems: heraldry of our own for each warband – do they fit?

  • Campaign

    Overview, battles, warbands; from here to the notes, timeline, world, background and management.
    • Does every tab lead where you expect?
    • World: districts with their effects and who holds them.
    • Manage ⚑: roles, invites, house rules, closing a battle.

  • Warband story

    Interludes and every warrior's life so far.
    • Is a warrior's life easy to find from his card (⋯ → His story so far)?

  • More

    Devices, authenticator, theme, reporting a problem, admin.
    • Is “Report a problem” easy to find?

  • Trading Post

    Buy, search for rare items, sell and hand over gear after the first battle (V4–V7).
    • Is it clear who may use an item, and why not?
    • Is the rare search readable without dice in the app?
    • Does every gold change show up in the ledger?
    • Tap an item: does its text say what it does?

  • Desktop

    A workspace of panels – the phone's screens, several at a time – for a wide screen.
    • Drag a panel by its title bar, or use its ⋯ menu: column left or right, up, down, across the full width, fill the workspace.
    • Pick a Hero in the Roster: the Warrior panel and the Trading Post follow.
    • Views: Roster, After battle, Trading, Story; your own arrangement can be kept as a view.
    • Home, Warbands, Campaign, Notes and More in the sidebar are workspaces of their own.
    • Calm enough, or too much at once?
